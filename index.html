Make it unavoidable on your phone or computer

A web page can't close or block other apps by itself. It can detect when you leave, track allowed-app time and make leaving costly. For a hard block, use your device's own tools with the same allowed list:

iPhone: Settings, Focus, choose Allowed Apps; Screen Time, App Limits.
Android: Settings, Digital Wellbeing, Focus mode and App timers.
Windows or Mac: a blocker such as Cold Turkey or Freedom, or the macOS Focus feature.
